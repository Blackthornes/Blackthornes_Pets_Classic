# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): the middle number
moves for new behaviour, the last for fixes alone. The newest version goes at
the top; a release is refused if its version has no section here.

## 0.5.0

- The minimap button has a new Blackthornes badge.
- Show on Map now puts a gold marker on your map.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.4.0

- One minimap button now opens all your Blackthornes add-ons.

## 0.3.1

- Various bug fixes.

## 0.3.0

- The small Feed button has a new gold look.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.2.0

- You can move the small Feed button by holding right-click and dragging it.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.1.0

First release for WoW Classic Era.

- **Feeding:** a Feed button and a warning when your pet gets hungry, and a list of the food in your bags your pet will eat.
- **Keys:** one key to call, revive or feed your pet, and one to dismiss it. Set them in the game's Key Bindings.
- **Pet Compendium:** every beast you can tame, with its family, level, zone and attack speed. Shift-click a beast and an arrow points you to it.
- **Skills card:** what your pet knows, what it can learn now, and what comes later. Hover a beast to see the skills taming it teaches.
- **For warlocks:** a Skills card for your demons, a soul shard count, and a reminder after a fight when you have no Healthstone or your Soulstone isn't on anyone. The shard warning and the reminder are off until you turn them on.
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch (German), French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
